BEFORE WE GET STARTED

A few good
questions.

Clear answers to help you feel confident about starting a software project.

Have another question?
Do I need to understand software to get started?+

No. Tell us how your business works and what is difficult to manage. You can show us a spreadsheet or describe a repeated task. We will help turn that into a clear list of requirements.

Do you work with clients outside India?+

Yes. We work remotely with businesses in India and internationally. We agree on communication, review milestones and a practical meeting schedule before starting.

Can you improve an existing Laravel application?+

Yes. We can investigate bugs, add features, review performance and help with maintenance. We start by understanding the codebase and the affected business workflow.

How do you estimate a project?+

We discuss your requirements, priorities, integrations and timeline. we then define the scope and assumptions before preparing a quotation. The initial project discussion is free.

Can you handle the complete application?+

Our work spans backend development, interfaces, database design, APIs and deployment. We agree on the exact responsibilities and deliverables for your project before development begins.

What happens after launch?+

Deployment, handover and support expectations are agreed as part of the project. Ongoing maintenance and new features can be scoped separately as your requirements evolve.

How long will the project take?+

The timeline depends on the features, the information we need to move and the tools we need to connect. After our first discussion, we agree on a realistic scope and schedule before development begins.

Can we talk if I do not have a fixed budget?+

Yes. Choose “Let’s discuss” on the enquiry form. We can identify the most useful first version and discuss its scope before we prepare a quotation. The initial discussion is free.
