Staff, attendance & payroll

Manage employee records, shifts, leave and salary calculations in one connected system (HRMS).

Request a project discussion

The business challenge

Attendance exceptions, changing shifts and different leave policies make payroll preparation time-consuming and difficult to verify.

How we approach it

We document policy rules and exception handling before development. Attendance, leave and payroll are connected through reviewable steps, with access controls for employee and salary information.

What we can build

  • Employee management
  • Biometric attendance
  • Leave and comp off
  • Shifts and timesheets
  • Payroll and salary templates
  • Payslips
  • Policies and notifications

How your project will work

We discuss what your team does today and which tasks the software should help with. Before work starts, we agree on the features, estimated cost, timeline and how you will review the work. We also discuss getting your team started and any support needed after launch.

Working with your team

We collaborate remotely with businesses in India and internationally. We agree on communication, review sessions and responsibilities early, so you can make informed decisions throughout the project.
