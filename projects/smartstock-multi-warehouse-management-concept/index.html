SmartStock – Multi-Warehouse Inventory Platform

A concept for multi-location businesses that need a clearer view of stock, purchasing, transfers and fulfilment.

Project typeConcept demonstration
IndustryRetail, distribution & warehousing
ArchitectureMulti-location inventory workflows
Proposed stackLaravel · PHP · MySQL · Vue.js · REST APIs

Concept overview

A concept for multi-location businesses that need a clearer view of stock, purchasing, transfers and fulfilment. This self-directed concept demonstrates how we would approach the product, workflows and technical structure; it is not presented as commissioned client work.

The business challenge

Growing distributors often manage stock through separate spreadsheets, calls and branch-level records. That makes it difficult to know what is available, what is already committed and where replenishment is needed.

The proposed solution

The SmartStock concept connects purchasing, receiving, storage, transfers, sales orders and dispatch around a traceable stock ledger. Role-based dashboards would give branch teams a focused workflow while management sees inventory across every location.

Capabilities demonstrated

  • Live stock by warehouse and location
  • Purchase orders and goods receiving
  • Sales orders and dispatch planning
  • Inter-warehouse stock transfers
  • Barcode-led receiving and picking
  • Low-stock and reorder alerts
  • Supplier and product management
  • Stock movement history and reports

Proposed technology stack

LaravelPHPMySQLVue.jsREST APIs

What this concept demonstrates

We designed this concept case study to demonstrate how we would structure multi-warehouse inventory, operational permissions and traceable stock movements in a custom Laravel application.

A closer look

Potential business value

A system like this could reduce duplicate entry, make stock discrepancies easier to investigate and help teams plan purchasing and fulfilment from a shared operational view.
