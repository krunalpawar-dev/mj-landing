StayPilot – Property Management SaaS

A concept SaaS workspace for property enquiries, bookings, tenants, payments, maintenance and owner reporting.

Project typeConcept demonstration
IndustryProperty rentals & hospitality
ArchitectureMulti-tenant property operations
Proposed stackLaravel · PHP · MySQL · Vue.js · Redis

Concept overview

A concept SaaS workspace for property enquiries, bookings, tenants, payments, maintenance and owner reporting. This self-directed concept demonstrates how we would approach the product, workflows and technical structure; it is not presented as commissioned client work.

The business challenge

Property managers can lose visibility when leads, booking calendars, tenant records, maintenance requests and payment follow-ups are handled in different tools.

The proposed solution

The StayPilot concept brings the customer journey and property operations together. A shared calendar would connect availability and bookings, while tenant, payment and maintenance records stay attached to the relevant unit and organization.

Capabilities demonstrated

  • Property and unit management
  • Lead and enquiry pipeline
  • Booking and occupancy calendar
  • Tenant profiles and agreements
  • Rent, deposits and payment tracking
  • Maintenance requests and assignments
  • Owner dashboards and statements
  • Multi-organization administration

Proposed technology stack

LaravelPHPMySQLVue.jsRedis

What this concept demonstrates

We designed this concept to demonstrate a multi-tenant SaaS architecture, role-based property operations and a joined-up workflow from initial enquiry through ongoing tenancy.

A closer look

Potential business value

A product like this could help property teams respond to enquiries sooner, reduce scheduling conflicts and give owners a clearer view of occupancy, collections and open maintenance work.
