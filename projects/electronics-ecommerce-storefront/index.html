Electronics Store – Product Discovery E-commerce Website

A catalogue-led electronics storefront with promotional collections, category browsing, product filters and customer enquiries.

TechnologiesPHP · MySQL · JavaScript · Bootstrap

Project overview

A catalogue-led electronics storefront with promotional collections, category browsing, product filters and customer enquiries. This case study outlines the application’s purpose, the functionality developed and the operational context behind the work.

The client problem

A broad electronics catalogue needs a clear way for shoppers to move from featured promotions into focused product browsing without losing access to categories, brands and availability filters.

The solution

We built a retail storefront that combines campaign-led landing content with a filterable shop grid. Product status, category and brand options help visitors narrow the catalogue, while contact and account areas support the wider customer journey.

Features developed

  • Promotional product collections
  • Category navigation
  • Product catalogue grid
  • Stock and sale filters
  • Brand-based browsing
  • Product ratings and pricing
  • Customer contact form
  • Responsive storefront layout

Technologies used

PHPMySQLJavaScriptBootstrap

Our contribution

We implemented the responsive storefront pages, catalogue navigation, filtering experience, promotional layouts and customer contact interface.

A closer look

Business impact

The website gives shoppers a structured way to explore a large catalogue and gives the retailer reusable spaces for promotions, categories and customer enquiries.
