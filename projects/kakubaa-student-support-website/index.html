Kakubaa – Student Support & Guidance Website

A responsive education website presenting academic guidance, student support services and consultation plans in one clear experience.

Project overview

A responsive education website presenting academic guidance, student support services and consultation plans in one clear experience. This case study outlines the application’s purpose, the functionality developed and the operational context behind the work.

The client problem

Kakubaa needed a welcoming public website that could explain its student guidance offering, present available plans and make it easy for students to start a conversation.

The solution

We created a bright, student-focused website with dedicated areas for services, plans, educational content, the team and contact options. Clear navigation and persistent WhatsApp access keep support within reach throughout the site.

Features developed

  • Student-focused landing page
  • Academic support services
  • Service and plan presentation
  • About and team pages
  • Blog content area
  • Contact enquiries
  • WhatsApp contact access
  • Responsive navigation

Business impact

The site gives students and families a single place to understand Kakubaa’s offering, compare support options and make an enquiry using the channel that suits them.
